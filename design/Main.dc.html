How it works

From postcode to pricing decision in three steps.

1

Tell us your business

Choose your sector, enter your postcode and how far your customers travel. Rivl finds the businesses you compete with.

2

See your local market

A map and ranked list of rivals’ prices for the same items, plus where you sit against the area low, median and high.

3

Get a monthly report

Who changed price, who opened nearby, and how your position moved, in your inbox every month with a quarterly review.

Decisions, not just data

Price with confidence when it counts.

  • Annual price review. Raise prices with evidence before costs force your hand.
  • New service. Price a new class, treatment or menu item against local rivals.
  • Second site. Compare price levels across postcodes before you expand.
  • New competitor. See their prices and how the local median shifts.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

Publicly listed prices from websites, menus and booking pages, plus prices businesses choose to share with Rivl.

Will competitors see my prices?

Prices you share only feed area averages. Rivl never shows your shared prices against your name.

How often is it updated?

Prices are refreshed regularly and summarised in a monthly report, with a deeper quarterly review.
